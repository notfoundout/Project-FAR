## 1. Design intent — not delivered

The clean-room read of Packet A discloses a residual structural hint: the A–I list tells the respondent which axes are worth separating. B2 removes that hint entirely. It asks the same substantive question with **no** sub-questions, no checklist, and no enumeration of what an answer should contain.

B2 keeps the criterion identical to Packet A — sameness in every respect that matters — and introduces no new substantive criterion.

The cost is that B2 output may be harder to map, because the respondent supplies their own structure. That is the point: whether the axes appear unprompted is the measurement.

**Interpretation limit.** If a B2 respondent does not address an axis, that is evidence they did not find it salient unprompted. It is **not** evidence that they would reject it. Absence under ablation is weaker than denial.

---

## 2. The question

Some systems take up a question or a problem and end at a conclusion, a judgement, or a decision.

Suppose one such episode is written down twice, in two different ways. What must the two write-ups have in common before they count as write-ups of the same episode in every respect that matters?

---

## 3. How to answer

Answer in whatever form and structure you judge appropriate. Set out your reasoning and your justification. There is no required format and no list of points to cover.

Record any counterexamples you built, approaches you abandoned, anything you regard as unresolved, and every source you used — marking each as a primary or original work, an authoritative technical reference, or a secondary or expository source.

Any conclusion you can justify is acceptable. The following are equally so, **in no particular order and not exhaustively** — a conclusion of a shape not listed is equally acceptable: that the question will not settle as put, that several rival answers are equally defensible, that different kinds of system need different answers, that one answer covers every case you admit, that one answer covers them given assumptions you state, or that you cannot reach a stable answer. **There is no target answer**, and none of these is preferred over another.
