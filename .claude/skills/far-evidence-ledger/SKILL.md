---
name: far-evidence-ledger
description: "Tracks the exact evidence supporting and opposing Project FAR claims, including source quality, independence, relevance, replication, counterexamples, and whether evidence actually discriminates among competing hypotheses."
---

Maintain an evidence ledger for Project FAR.

Every substantive claim must be connected to specific evidence.

For each evidence item record:

- evidence ID
- claim(s) affected
- source
- evidence type
- direct or indirect
- supporting or opposing
- independent or derivative
- reproducible or non-reproducible
- discriminating power
- limitations
- confidence impact

Evidence types may include:
- formal proof
- countermodel
- empirical observation
- framework embedding
- literature result
- computational test
- conceptual argument
- failed replication
- negative result
- expert assertion

Do not treat all evidence equally.

Apply these checks:

1. Does the evidence directly bear on the claim?
2. Does it discriminate between competing hypotheses?
3. Is it independent of evidence already counted?
4. Does it rely on the claim's own definitions?
5. Can it be reproduced or reconstructed?
6. Does it test the strongest version of the claim?
7. Is it positive evidence or merely absence of failure?
8. Does contradictory evidence exist?
9. Would the claim survive removal of this evidence?

Do not double-count multiple documents deriving from the same underlying result.

Do not convert repetition into independent support.

Preserve negative evidence.

If evidence is insufficient, say so explicitly.
