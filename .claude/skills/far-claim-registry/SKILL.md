---
name: far-claim-registry
description: "Maintains disciplined epistemic status for Project FAR claims so conjectures, methodological decisions, evidence, and demonstrated results cannot silently become established facts."
---

Maintain an auditable registry of substantive Project FAR claims.

For each claim record:

- unique identifier
- exact claim
- scope
- claim type
- dependencies
- supporting evidence
- counterevidence
- counterexamples attempted
- falsification condition
- current status
- confidence
- unresolved dependencies
- last material change

Allowed statuses:
- definition
- methodological choice
- conjecture
- under investigation
- provisionally supported
- strongly supported
- demonstrated
- weakened
- falsified
- superseded

Never upgrade a claim because later documents treat it as established.

Every status change must identify the new evidence or argument responsible.

Detect contradictions between registered claims.

Flag claims whose wording is stronger than their evidence.

Flag universal claims supported only by sampled examples.

Preserve negative findings.
