## Question

Can Project FAR construct a non-self-activating **Research candidate** evidence-authority contract that:

1. assigns proposition-specific owner classes and scopes without treating repository location, CI success, formal notation, repetition, or index inclusion as truth;
2. records every governing dependency needed to interpret the contract, including the research-execution charter and proof-assurance taxonomy;
3. identifies every proof artifact registered by the repository's current JSON, YAML, Markdown, and Lean metadata pathways;
4. requires explicit charter status and authority-bearing designation before any proof artifact can carry active proof authority;
5. prevents the model, registry, status manifest, or governance log from accepting or promoting themselves;
6. sends unresolved equal-priority contradictions to `Unknown` pending an independently authorized governance decision; and
7. remains unable to authorize Acceptance, Promotion, canonical dependency reconciliation, or experiment execution while its bootstrap authority is `Unknown`?

## Candidate outcomes

- `bounded_candidate_supported`: at least one contract satisfies every preregistered construction and falsification condition, but remains Research and inactive.
- `candidate_refuted`: every tested contract violates at least one condition.
- `unknown`: source identity, source scope, proof-path completeness, or conflict behavior cannot be verified from the frozen design.

## Nonclaims

It does not ask whether FAR or FARO is logically derived from FARA. It does not authorize an experiment or repository-wide status transition.
