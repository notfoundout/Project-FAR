# Neutral Case-Selection and Clean-Room Derivation Protocol v1.3

Status: **Research**  
Program ID: `TCD-CLEANROOM-001`  
Version: 1.3  
Supersedes within this program: v1.2  
Execution state: **blocked**

## 1. Research question

What review questions are required to evaluate explicitly auditable reasoning artifacts, and—only after those questions are frozen—what information structures answer them with the least registered total machinery?

The protocol does not ask any participant to recover RCCD, FAR, FARA, FARO, a fixed factor count, or any named architecture.

## 2. Evidence phases

### Stage 0 — Source and packet operations

Stage 0 instantiates sources, captures exact bytes, records lawful access, applies the frozen source-selection protocol, creates blind packets, runs leakage review, and commits the restricted package. It produces no theory evidence.

### Stage A1 — Primary undisclosed-failure derivation

The primary packet contains the task environment, artifact, supported output, reviewer role, source-native objective, access limits, answer constraints, and source-native rules necessary to understand the task. It does **not** disclose curator-known failure scenarios, expected audit questions, target terminology, candidate fields, or structural hypotheses.

Each development case receives three context-isolated runs. Their evidential label is only `context_isolated` unless stronger independence is separately documented.

### Stage A2 — Disclosed-failure completeness arm

After all A1 outputs are sealed, a separate workspace may receive the sealed curator failure ledger and ask whether A1 omitted any objective-linked failure. A2 is a completeness comparison, not independent discovery. New questions found only in A2 are labeled `curator_prompted` and may not be counted as A1 convergence.

### Stage A3 — Adjudication

Adjudication follows `stage-a-adjudication-codebook-v1.0.md`. The adjudicator may not invent a question, silently alter an answer space, or merge questions outside the codebook. Every decision is reproducible from immutable inputs.

### Stage B — Structural analysis

Only after the Stage A ledger is frozen may separately isolated analysts propose sufficient information sets, paired necessity witnesses, exact recodings, hidden-reintroduction checks, alternative bases, and costs. Costing follows `stage-b-cost-instrument-v1.0.md`.

### Validation and challenge

Validation identities remain sealed until Stage A, Stage B, scoring, cost rules, and failure rules are frozen. Challenge cases are revealed last. Profile-known allocation is disclosed as a limitation: source identities are hidden, but dimension profiles are public.

## 3. Packet schema

The primary A1 packet contains exactly:

1. blind packet ID;
2. task environment;
3. artifact;
4. supported output;
5. reviewer role;
6. source-native review objective;
7. source-native rules required for task interpretation;
8. permitted external sources and access limits;
9. answer constraints and uncertainty rules.

The A1 packet must not contain:

- known or suspected failure scenarios;
- expected questions or answer fields;
- target architecture terminology;
- proposed primitives or modules;
- requested factor counts;
- Stage B instructions;
- source identity unless indispensable and approved by leakage review;
- development/validation labels.

The curator failure ledger is a separate restricted artifact and is unavailable until A2.

## 4. Source selection

All real-source selection follows `source-selection-and-replacement-protocol-v1.0.md`. No case is instantiated from an ad hoc search. The eligible source universe, query set, retrieval window, ordering rule, stopping rule, candidate log, coding review, exclusion rule, and replacement chain must be frozen before final selection.

No source used to design the target architecture or these controls is eligible for the 36-case sample.

## 5. Source and synthetic freeze

A real case requires record-level identity, exact captured bytes, per-file SHA-256, retrieval metadata, access/license note, eligibility decision, dimension rationale, candidate-log pointer, and replacement history.

A synthetic case requires a deterministic generator or complete manual specification, exact version, seed when applicable, generated bytes, per-file SHA-256, and passing/failing mutation witnesses.

A URL, report family, seed plus prose, or mutable workspace is not a frozen case.

## 6. Restricted package commitment

Restricted files are committed by the deterministic format in `restricted-package-format-v1.0.md`. Canonical path normalization, byte hashing, sorted entries, symlink rejection, deterministic JSON serialization, and a Merkle root are mandatory. A mutable ZIP digest alone is insufficient.

## 7. Roles and access

Operational roles, prohibited combinations, conflict declarations, access grants, substitutions, and reveal permissions follow `role-access-conflict-matrix-v1.0.md`.

A role label without a named assignee, signed conflict declaration, credential boundary, and access-log entry is uninstantiated. Uninstantiated roles keep execution blocked.

## 8. Stage A output

Each A1 run returns:

- review-task restatement;
- failure-mode ledger;
- audit-question ledger;
- answer spaces;
- permitted evidence;
- access/oracle limits;
- Unknown meaning;
- failure predicates;
- unresolved facts;
- contamination declaration.

Each question must be linked to a source-native objective or independently derived failure mode. Stage A may not propose architecture, minimum information sets, recodings, ablations, or alternatives.

## 9. Adjudication and freeze

The codebook fixes normalization, equivalence, merge, recurrence, evidence, disagreement, and status rules. Before Stage B, freeze:

- every raw A1 output;
- the curator ledger;
- every A2 output;
- adjudication records;
- accepted, provisional, disputed, and rejected questions;
- answer spaces and failure predicates;
- contamination and deviations.

A post-exposure codebook change creates a new protocol version and preserves the prior result.

## 10. Stage B

For each frozen accepted or provisional question:

1. provide one or more sufficient information sets;
2. provide an explicit reconstruction procedure;
3. attempt paired-witness ablation;
4. inspect renamed, hidden, external, or interpreter-based reintroduction;
5. test registered exact recodings;
6. generate coverage, Pareto, and robustness bases;
7. score machinery only through the frozen cost instrument;
8. preserve incomparable and Unknown outcomes.

No weighting rule is permitted unless frozen before validation. Absent a weighting rule, only coordinate-wise Pareto claims are allowed.

## 11. Exact freeze controls

The canonical public design is locked by exact SHA-256 digests in `freeze-manifest-v1.0.json`. Semantic verification is necessary but not sufficient. Any byte change to a governed artifact requires:

- a new artifact version;
- an updated freeze manifest;
- a documented reason;
- complete revalidation;
- a new exposure boundary when exposure has occurred.

`verify_program_freeze.py` checks the manifest against repository bytes. `verify_sampling_design.py` separately checks the design semantics and exact CSV/report identities.

## 12. Sacrificial operational pilot

Before selecting the 36 cases, execute `sacrificial-operational-pilot-v1.0.md` on three fixtures permanently excluded from development, validation, and challenge evidence. The pilot tests capture, hashing, deterministic commitment, sanitation, access logging, release, revocation, and recovery only.

Pilot success authorizes source-freeze operations, not Stage A derivation.

## 13. Stopping and invalidation

Stop and mark the version invalid when:

- a governed artifact changes without a new freeze;
- an A1 packet discloses curator-known failures;
- a derivator accesses restricted identities or prior outputs;
- adjudication departs from the codebook;
- cost scoring uses an unregistered unit or weight;
- a source is selected outside the frozen search procedure;
- restricted-package reproduction fails;
- role or access evidence is incomplete;
- validation is revealed before the freeze gate.

Failure is retained. It is not silently repaired.

## 14. Current gate

Protocol defects identified in the 2026-08-05 follow-up audit are corrected in this version. Execution remains blocked until:

- role assignments and conflict declarations exist;
- a restricted custodian store is operational;
- the sacrificial pilot passes;
- source universes and queries are instantiated;
- all 36 sources/synthetics are frozen;
- packet and leakage review are complete.

No clean-room derivation is authorized.
