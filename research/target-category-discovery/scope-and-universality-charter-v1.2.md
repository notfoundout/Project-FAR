## 2. Formal scope

The preserved exploratory argument ranges over all small categories and all functors. Its operation schema uses a fixed finite directed graph with distinguished source and target vertices and returns one arrow between their images.

The candidate argument says invariant operations in that scope are path evaluations in the free category. Identity paths are structural terms when source equals target. Sequential composition supplies the committed nonidentity witness `b∘a:A->C`.

This argument does not choose a universal domain of reasoning or a common comparison order over categories, bare sets, semicategories, semigroupoids, higher categories, probabilistic systems, embodied systems, continuous systems, or oracle-dependent systems.
