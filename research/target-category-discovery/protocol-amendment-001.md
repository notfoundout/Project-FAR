# Protocol Amendment 001 — Primary Sample Size and Pairwise Design

Status: **Research**  
Program ID: `TCD-CLEANROOM-001`  
Effective before any derivation: yes

## Original design

The generated clean-room protocol proposed:

- 18 development cases;
- eight validation cases;
- 26 primary cases total.

It also required complete pairwise coverage across six review-objective levels and six artifact-medium levels.

## Defect

Each case occupies one review-objective/artifact-medium cell. The cross-product contains:

`6 × 6 = 36` cells.

A 26-row design cannot contain all 36 cells. The original sample size was therefore inconsistent with its own coverage requirement.

## Amendment

The public primary design is changed to:

- 24 development rows;
- 12 sealed validation rows;
- 36 primary rows total;
- six separate adversarial challenge cases.

The resulting mixed-level array covers 434/434 required cross-dimension level pairs.

## Claim boundary

The amendment proves only that 36 rows are required by the selected objective/medium full-cross criterion and that the registered array satisfies the selected pairwise criterion.

It does not prove that 36 is statistically sufficient, representative, minimal for every possible covering array criterion, or adequate for universality claims.

## Source status

This amendment applies to row assignments only. Source-case instantiation remains incomplete and must satisfy Protocol v1.2 before execution.
