# Public Sampling Design v1.1

Status: **Research**  
Program ID: `TCD-CLEANROOM-001`  
Design version: 1.1  
Design seed: `20260805`

## 1. Current status

The 36-row **covering-array design** is verified. The source cases and packets are not yet fully instantiated or frozen.

No row in the public design should be interpreted as an eligible, sanitized, released, or independently validated case.

## 2. Allocation

- 24 development rows;
- 12 sealed validation rows;
- six separate adversarial challenge cases, not included in the covering array.

The original 26-primary-case proposal was amended because six review objectives crossed with six artifact media create 36 required pairs. A design with 26 rows cannot cover all 36 of those pairs when each row supplies only one objective/medium combination.

## 3. Dimensions

The public design crosses seven dimensions.

### Output type — 5 levels

- Factual or analytic conclusion
- Classification
- Recommendation
- Plan
- Authorization or decision

### Governing relation — 5 levels

- Explicit rule or deduction
- Evidential or probabilistic assessment
- Causal or counterfactual assessment
- Optimization or multi-criteria choice
- Sequential or interactive procedure

### Temporal structure — 3 levels

- Static snapshot
- Ordered sequence
- Path-sensitive history

### Information condition — 4 levels

- Materially complete information
- Explicit uncertainty
- Missing information
- Conflicting information

### Primary review objective — 6 levels

- Support or correctness
- Reproducibility
- Authorization or compliance
- Sensitivity or robustness
- Provenance or accountability
- Comparison with an alternative

### Artifact medium — 6 levels

- Narrative
- Table or form
- Graph or diagram
- Executable rule, query, or program
- Event log or timeline
- Hybrid

### Domain source — 3 levels

- Naturally occurring public case
- De-identified operational case
- Neutral synthetic case

```

Expected terminal result:

```

## 5. Exact interpretation of coverage

The result means only:

> Every pair of levels from two different declared dimensions appears in at least one row.

It does not mean:

- the dimensions are exhaustive;
- the cases represent a population;
- the sample is statistically powered;
- the case coding is correct;
- the selected sources are independent;
- every relevant reasoning domain is represented;
- a structure recurring in the sample is universal;
- a missing structure is unnecessary.

## 6. Public-versus-restricted separation

The public CSV contains only:

- row number;
- blind case ID;
- allocation;
- seven dimension values.

It deliberately excludes:

- source titles;
- organizations;
- URLs;
- domains;
- source bytes;
- source hashes;
- synthetic seeds;
- internal project IDs;
- packet hashes;
- validation identities.

Those records remain in a restricted custodian store under [`restricted-artifacts-policy.md`](restricted-artifacts-policy.md).

## 7. Source-instantiation audit

The generated v1.0 source roster did not meet freeze requirements.

Material deficiencies were:

1. at least one operational row identified a report set rather than one record;
2. synthetic rows were descriptions plus seeds rather than generated frozen artifacts;
3. mutable URLs were recorded without byte-level snapshots;
4. source matching was curator judgment and was not generated by a reproducible selection algorithm;
5. eligibility and leakage review had not been completed;
6. the document called the cases frozen when only the combinatorial row assignments were frozen.

Therefore source status is:

`incomplete — not frozen — not released`.

## 8. Allocation limitation

All 12 synthetic rows are development rows. The validation rows contain six naturally occurring public cases and six de-identified operational cases.

This design choice creates a source-class/allocation dependency. Under the present design:

- held-out success may support bounded transfer to the public and operational source classes represented;
- held-out success cannot establish generalization to synthetic cases as a holdout class;
- differences between development and validation may be partly attributable to source class;
- source-stratified results must be reported.

Changing this allocation requires a new sampling-design version, not a silent replacement.

## 9. Source matching and replacement

For each row, the curator must identify a case matching all seven assigned levels. The curator must record:

- why each level applies;
- every rejected candidate;
- every replacement;
- whether judgment was required;
- any ambiguity in coding.

If no eligible source matches a row, the result is an unfilled row. The dimensions may not be changed merely to fit an available source.

## 10. Freeze conditions

The source roster can be declared frozen only after every row has:

- a record-level identity;
- a retrieval date;
- captured bytes or a stable archival package;
- SHA-256 digest;
- access and license note;
- eligibility pass;
- dimension coding rationale;
- source-to-packet plan;
- no unresolved placeholder.

A synthetic row also requires the complete generator/specification and generated artifact hash.

## 11. Challenge set

The six adversarial challenges remain outside the development and validation covering array. They are revealed only after development synthesis and scoring rules are frozen.

They test:

- absence versus explicit negative information;
- path-sensitive differences behind the same final output;
- lossless representational recoding;
- conflicting sources or rules;
- hidden oracle/environment dependence;
- smaller equal-behavior alternatives.

They are challenge evidence, not independent recurrence evidence.

## 12. Adoption boundary

Version 1.1 freezes only the public row-level combinatorial design and its verification rules.

It does not freeze or accept the source roster, packets, synthetic artifacts, audit questions, requirement basis, RCCD mapping, or comparison result.
