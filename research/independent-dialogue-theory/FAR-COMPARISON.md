## Where they touch

FAR's boundary specification assigns to **FARA** the layer owning *"representational objects,
states, transitions, admissibility architecture."* `FARA-FORMAL-KERNEL-001` is recorded there as
an *identity-bearing many-sorted relational carrier architecture with typed occurrence-sensitive
identity*, and it records that *"typed-hypergraph and algebraic/state-transition forms remain
derived views rather than upstream prerequisites."*

SSS lives on that terrain, and two of its moves are the same moves:

1. **State type versus transition structure.** SSS-1/2/3 are indexed by *state type* and show
   that sufficiency is a property of the (state type, transition structure) pair, not of either
   alone. In particular SSS-2 identifies a case where the **hypergraph form is the sufficient
   one over a given state type** while the binary form over that same state type is not — a
   remark about the relationship between two of the forms FARA's kernel note names.
2. **Occurrence-sensitivity.** SSS-1's frontier construction works because open leaves are
   *occurrences*, so duplicate pending sequents remain distinct and a multiset is the right
   image. FARA's kernel independently records typed occurrence-sensitive identity as
   constitutive.

That is genuine partial overlap on the question *when is a state/transition representation
sufficient for what it represents*.

**Narrower than Revision 1 claimed.** Revision 1 asserted an adequacy *criterion* with an "iff".
That is withdrawn. What remains are two sufficiency results and one impossibility bounded by an
explicit representation class and an explicit decoder class — outside which it is false. The
overlap with FARA is correspondingly narrower: SSS offers no characterisation that any FARA
claim could be measured against.

## Where they do not touch

- **Provenance is independent.** SSS was derived inside a closed dialogue-built experiment whose
  panel, grammars, source rules and prediction were frozen without reference to FAR, and whose
  results include the refutation of its own registered hypothesis (DF-04-H) and of its own
  preregistered prediction (A2). No FAR artifact was used as a premise; nothing in SSS was
  derived from FARA.
- **Scope is incomparable.** SSS-3 is proved for MLL over two named classes; SSS-1 and SSS-2 are
  elementary and almost certainly folklore. FARA's kernel is recorded as Accepted for *"Project
  FAR v1.0 finite explicit auditable representational architecture."* Neither range contains the
  other, and SSS is by far the narrower.
- **SSS says nothing about FAR's central result.** The repository records a relative,
  operational, strictly-weakened universality theorem `POST-TUE-UPP-001`, with a bounded-v1
  cross-audit adjudicated `FROZEN_V1_NOT_REFUTED_BUT_NOT_ESTABLISHED` and a registered successor
  repair program. SSS neither supports nor challenges any of it — not the UPP premises, the
  dependency audit, the maximality boundary, nor the repair program.
- **No transfer of status in either direction.** SSS's proofs are internal, OCR-assisted in one
  case, and unreviewed; they confer nothing on FAR. FAR's repository status confers nothing on
  SSS. Repository placement is not epistemic authority in either direction.
- **E1's common-content kernel transfers nothing.** `J-star`, the 88-item successor registry,
  and $\forall x\neg(x\to x)$ are Research-status, profile-relative results with no
  JOINT-COMPLETE or novelty certificate. They neither instantiate RCCD nor constrain any FAR
  artifact.

## One observation, offered without any claim of authority

The concrete procedure that did work here — take a frozen target's stated state type, enumerate
the structures compatible with it, and discharge each against a *preregistered* admissibility
condition rather than against mathematical definability alone — is what removed a wrong verdict
(Revision 1's S6 UNDERDETERMINED) and produced a right one. If it survives independent review it
would be a *candidate* audit pattern for representational-adequacy claims of the kind FARA's
layer makes.

Offered as a possible future test, not as a finding, not as a dependency, and not as evidence
for or against anything registered in Project FAR. Acting on it would require the registration,
preregistration and adversarial-gate machinery governing Project FAR research, none of which has
been invoked here.
