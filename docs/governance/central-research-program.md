# Central Research Program

Status: **Accepted**

## Purpose

Project FAR's broad theory question is terminal. The governing result is [Project FAR Core Theory v1.1](../../theory/theorems/Project-FAR-Theory-Closure-v1.1.md), a governed correction of the immutable historical v1.0 artifact.

The project no longer seeks one contract-free universal primitive inventory. It develops and audits contract-relative representations: cases, tests, contexts, typed outcomes, semantics, transformations, profiles, frames, and loss/cost objectives are declared before adequacy or minimality is judged.

## Terminal answer

- **Contract-free branch:** on a nontrivial domain, no single representation is simultaneously least-informative sufficient for every observation contract. This does not deny that identity can be sufficient for every fixed-domain contract.
- **Contract-relative branch:** exact sufficiency is equivalent to behavior factorization, and every fixed exact contract induces a unique least-informative observational quotient up to isomorphism.
- **Common-content branch:** exact common theory is indexed by language, interpretation profiles/models, and target class; its frame-subtracted substantive residue is additionally indexed by the interface frame.
- **Primitive/operator branch:** global counts are not invariant under faithful reification, tagging, splitting, or combination.
- **Finite-evidence branch:** finite panels establish only panel-relative results unless a separate theorem covers the remainder.

The central theory is Accepted as internal deductive work corrected after a non-independent hostile audit and subsequently confirmed by a separate sealed independent review under its exact scopes. That review found 14 `PROVED`, no adverse or unresolved claim verdicts, and no required correction; it did not establish novelty or priority.

## Program architecture after closure

- **Shared theory:** `FAR-CORE-001` through `FAR-CORE-014`.
- **FARA:** the selected finite explicit auditable v1 representation target, not a universal source ontology.

- **FARO:** execution, materialized views, comparison, audit, and reporting.
- **Post-closure assurance/application:** governed by `POST-CLOSURE-001`.

## Proof and evidence discipline

- Preserve exact scope, premises, contract, profile, frame, outcome types, decoder, and falsifier.
- For `FAR-CORE-010`, distinguish exact `T_{L,J,I}` from the `Γ`-relative frame-subtracted residue.
- Distinguish mathematical proof, executable corroboration, schema conformance, empirical observation, and independent review.
- A decoder/factorization proof supports exact sufficiency; a collision refutes it; otherwise return `OPEN`.
- Determinate absence, falsity, inapplicability, failure, unresolvedness, and epistemic `Unknown` remain distinct whenever the contract distinguishes them.
- Encoding capacity is not native common architecture.
- Historical evidence is never silently rewritten to match later theory.

## Reopening and change control

Only a reproducible contradiction to `FAR-CORE-001`–`FAR-CORE-014`, their stated proofs, or their formal premises may reopen the minimum affected core claims. A stronger scope, approximate theorem, categorical generalization, or cost-minimality theorem requires its own explicit premises and claim identity.
