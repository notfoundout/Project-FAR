# Expanded bounded FARA campaign

Status: Research

## Question

Do the bounded core-formalization and foundation-comparison observations persist when the carrier ceiling is expanded beyond two?

## Execution and observation

`python tools/check_fara_expanded_campaign.py --write` independently enumerated unary and binary relation axes for carrier sizes 0 through 4, rebuilt the core proof, and rebuilt all 57 foundation mappings, 21 ablations (336 benchmark executions), 12 round-trip records, preservation matrices, structural accounts, and Pareto vectors. The axes examined 31 unary and 66,067 binary interpretations. Cross-products of all signature relations were deliberately not enumerated and are outside the evidence.

Every required target has paired-reduct countermodels at each tested size. The earlier bounded non-derivability observations therefore persist only through size 4 on the declared independent axes. The foundation comparison again leaves multiple foundations Pareto-incomparable. Preservation failures and partial results remain present; nothing was promoted based on the earlier campaign.

Oracle, continuous, hybrid, and embodied cases remain Unknown because this campaign supplies no executable semantics for them.

## Discovery and acceptance boundary

This is reproducible Research evidence, not accepted theory. Historical size-at-most-two artifacts remain byte-preserved and are hash-checked by the new validator. The generated report, proof object, and 57 trace manifests are distinct generated evidence.

No universality, uniqueness, global superiority, minimality, necessity, or completeness claim is made. No workstream label is introduced.
