Status: **Research**

## Authority and scope

## Method

The campaign freezes seventeen direction-aware Pareto dimensions before execution and does not weight or average them. It executes 19 source-system benchmarks against three separate Python representations and evaluators. Translation and reconstruction are explicit stages: every one of the 57 cases records source and generated elements, a correspondence, omissions and introductions, native versus derived structure, external assumptions, reconstruction and execution steps, and a six-dimensional preservation vector.

The corpus includes deterministic and probabilistic transition, nonmonotonic, paraconsistent, causal, rule-changing, interpretation-changing, ontology, merge, deletion, provenance, partial-order, proof-identity, binding, authority, oracle, continuous/hybrid, and embodied cases. The last three remain `Unknown` because the frozen campaign has no executable live-oracle, nonfinite continuous, or environment-inclusive semantics.

## Results

Each candidate achieves exact reconstruction on the benchmark families structurally native to its frozen signature and incurs at least one historical or identity distortion when the relevant structure is encoded. Equal terminal outputs are never treated as commitment equivalence. The many-sorted candidate is exposed by extensional duplicate identity and derived composition; the hypergraph candidate by derived path meaning and external rule interpretation; the algebraic candidate by non-native source/representation separation and quotient-sensitive provenance.

The executable paired-model ledger retains three non-equivalence witnesses. Cardinality blocks an isomorphism between one extensional tuple and two identity-bearing parallel edges. Native-symbol preservation blocks identifying primitive operation composition with an absent path-derived composite. Sort preservation blocks reconstructing a primitive source/token separation from an undifferentiated carrier without a material extension. Each repair is explicitly charged.

Ablations remove seven major constructs from every candidate. They establish only bounded implementation dependence: simulation through a catch-all is rejected, while a declared derived repair is charged. Ten counterexample searches preserve both successful witnesses and unresolved attempts. Bias records identify each benchmark's source formalism, closest candidate, auxiliary ancestry, representation-neutral variant, and order/naming mutation result.

## Adjudication

The exact terminal result is: **multiple foundations remain Pareto-incomparable**.

The dominance graph has three nodes and no edges. Every pair has opposed frozen dimensions: native exactness and lower repair cost in one area trade against identity, historical, semantic, operational, or structural cost elsewhere. No candidate is no worse on every dimension. This is not a forced tie and does not select a preferred foundation.

The strongest supported finding is bounded: all three frozen foundations are coherent enough to execute part of the finite corpus, none has a justified componentwise advantage over both others, and absent external/nonfinite semantics prevents stronger adjudication.

## Refuted and unresolved claims

The campaign refutes, within the frozen implementations, output equality as sufficient for commitment equivalence and prose-only equivalence among the three signatures. It also detects repairs that materially import another candidate's native machinery.

It does not resolve which identity criteria match intended canonical FARA, whether independent implementations reproduce the matrix, or how live oracles, continuous dynamics, and embodied environments should be modeled. A neutral external corpus and a complete formal model class for the old prose theory remain absent.

## Nonclaims

No result establishes canonical uniqueness, global superiority, universal representation, global minimality, primitive necessity, completeness, or universality. The campaign does not prove any construct globally necessary and does not promote finite observations beyond the frozen contract.
