## C5. Independent prior-art conclusions

1. The mathematical kernel of 001–003 is a standard quotient/factorization and
   behavior-preserving abstraction pattern, with close predecessors in several
   fields.
2. The universal-representation obstruction in 004 is correct as a simple
   two-contract result under its stated nontrivial-domain and contract-richness
   hypotheses.  Its broad philosophical reading has much stronger domain prior
   art and is not established as novel by this review.
3. Claim 005 is essentially definitional and has a direct classical predecessor.
4. Claims 006–008 formalize familiar distinctions between underlying-set codes,
   structure-preserving maps, and presentation-dependent signatures/bases.
5. Claim 009 is an elementary non-entailment.  It must not be confused with a
   denial of statistically justified generalization under declared assumptions.
6. Claim 010 is a dependency audit of definitions standard in model theory.
7. Claims 011–013 overlap exact-view determinacy and materialized-view theory;
   the application-specific premise in 013 is not supplied by that literature.
8. The exact phrase “Search-State Sufficiency” produced no relevant scholarly
   hit in one search.  This neither proves novelty nor changes the locked scope
   of 014.

No claim receives a proof merely because the literature contains an analogous
result.  Conversely, no adverse novelty finding refutes a correct theorem.  The
Stage-D verdict will be based on the exact reconstructed obligations and the
strongest countermodels, with this corpus used only to expose assumptions and
overclaim risks.
