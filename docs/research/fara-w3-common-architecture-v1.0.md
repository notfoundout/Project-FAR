## Authority recovery and discrepancy

Repository authority asks whether structured reasoning **can be represented** by a common architecture. It explicitly says FARA is a representation and analysis architecture, not a prescription for how reasoning must occur. The prompt's “common architecture across structured reasoning systems” is broader if it means a native or necessary constitution. W3 therefore follows repository authority: it tests representational existence and six-dimensional preservation, while separating native structure, necessity, uniqueness, and universality.

W0 is preserved. W1 remains a completed fail-closed execution in which all seven candidate-primitive independence outcomes are unresolved. W2 remains only coordinate-separation-induced bounded minimality in `finite_coordinate_trace_v1`. W3 neither reinterprets nor weakens those upstream results.

## Exact proposition

`P_W3_CTC`: for every member `S` of `C_W3`, there exists a configuration-transition-context trace reconstruction `M(S)` preserving structural, semantic, operational, dependency, information, and historical observations under `E_W3`, using only machinery declared in the family record.

- `C_W3` is exactly the ten specimens recorded in the proof object. It is an adversarial challenge set, not “all reasoning systems” and not a representative sample.
- `CTC=(configurations, typed transitions, declared context, observation map, history order)`.
- `E_W3` requires equality of each specimen's independently declared observations in all six dimensions. Ambiguous, assumed, or missing equality does not pass.
- Typed payloads, measures, calculi, equations, clocks, environment models, versioned interpreters, event structures, and oracles may be used only when declared and charged. Their use cannot support a claim that CTC is native, necessary, unique, minimal, or explanatory.

## Fail-closed adjudication

The strongest established claim is only existential reconstruction for `B_W3_closed_explicit`: finite or finitely presented, closed, explicitly specified systems whose complete declared observables and determinants are supplied as typed payloads and whose histories are totally ordered or have a declared linearization. Even there, success is by construction and does not show native common structure.

Uniqueness is refuted because behaviorally equivalent trace, quotient, and event-structure presentations are available. Lossless universal reconstruction from explicit internal state alone is refuted by the oracle-paired countermodel. A non-vacuous architecture covering all ten specimens, necessity, minimality, and open-world universality remain unresolved. Five favorable bounded encodings cannot promote the result.
