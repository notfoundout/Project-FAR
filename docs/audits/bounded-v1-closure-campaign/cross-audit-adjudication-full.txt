## 3. W9 Countermodel Adjudication

Exact proposition at issue. UPP-R3-DEPENDENCY-NECESSITY-001 (theory/foundation/upp-dependency-structure-v1.0.json): under seven antecedents — target_class_member, representation_admissible, machinery_closed, fully_faithful, equivalence_preserved, dependency_queries_total, failure_unknown_separated — a recoverable typed dependency relation exists, where the frozen positive-result witness requirements include "nonempty typed dependency edge set" and "at least one support or defeat edge exists" (JSON witness_requirements; doc: "The witness is a nonempty directed relation… A positive theorem result additionally requires at least one support or defeat edge").

Do the antecedents imply (A) nonemptiness and (B) a support/defeat edge? No, on both counts:

- C* (upp-target-class-v1.0.json): the five criteria are behavioral; appraisal_sensitive_response is satisfiable through "changing the supplied grounds or standard", and normative_appraisability is a disjunction whose "permitted/prohibited under an explicitly supplied standard" branch requires no inter-commitment support. No criterion mentions dependency edges.
- E* (E1–E6): finiteness/effectivity/inventory/totality/identity — no edge forced.
- P*: dependency_fidelity asks whether registered support/defeat/revision questions "can be correctly answered". A truthful "none/empty" answer is correct. Decisively, information_fidelity requires recovery to "avoid both omission and fabrication" — a faithful representation of a dependency-free system is forbidden from exposing a support/defeat edge. The clause-independence witness for dependency fidelity ("fabricated support and defeat relations" as a failure case) confirms fabrication is a P* violation.
- W5 closure / W6 equivalence: dependencies_preserved is trivially satisfied by preserving the empty relation bijectively. No forcing.
- No other frozen proposition (W1 foundation, definitions, glossary) classifies an appraisal standard as a support or defeat edge. Dismissing C-D1 by redefining "standard" as an edge would be exactly the post-hoc redefinition the frozen definitions do not license (and even an "admissibility"-kind edge would not satisfy the support-or-defeat requirement).

Premise-by-premise test of C-D1 (finite threshold adjudicator; two responses; explicit standard; standard-sensitive; total registered dependency queries all truthfully answered "empty"): C* — pass (all five criteria); E* — pass (finite explicit package, effective, inventoried, total, stable); P* — pass on all eight clauses (empty dependency answers are the correct answers; fabrication would fail); machinery closure — pass (trivial disclosed machinery); W6 — pass (empty relation round-trips exactly); dependency_queries_total — pass; failure_unknown_separated — pass. Every antecedent is satisfiable and satisfied.

Conclusion test. The positive witness fails: no nonempty edge set and no support/defeat edge exists to recover, and none may be fabricated. The frozen executable model corroborates this exactly: assess_dependency_structure with all premises YES and edges=() returns UNKNOWN (never PROVED), and the frozen test suite pins this behavior by name — test_empty_edges_are_unknown_not_proved. So the frozen chain itself demonstrates that W9 cannot deliver its positive witness on this antecedent-satisfying corner; yet the workstream's terminal result records the necessity lemma as universally "established relative to" the frozen premises, and W12/W13/W15 consume it in that universal form (W12's distinctive_obligations.dependency_structure includes support_or_defeat_edge).

Secondary finding. The model's classification of a positively established empty relation as UNKNOWN conflates determinate absence with unresolved evidence, in tension with the frozen error/Unknown separation discipline (P* error_and_unknown_separation; W9's own anti-trivialization "failure may not be collapsed into Unknown"). The frozen vocabulary cannot express "established-empty".

Empty-but-typed relation question. Yes: W9's JSON statement field ("a recoverable typed dependency relation exists") is already satisfied by an empty typed, query-total, equivalence-stable relation; only the witness-requirement pair excludes it. Permitting an explicitly established-empty typed relation is the mathematically honest weakening (see §14), but it produces a revised lemma — it is not entailed by the frozen positive-witness formulation.

## 8. P*/W6 Conclusion-Loading Adjudication

Proposition-by-proposition comparison: P*'s eight clauses (and W6's ten dimensions) impose informational preservation requirements — recoverability of registered structural facts, meanings, operational distinctions, dependence answers, provenance/path identity, query totality, error/Unknown separation. The five RCCD components are recoverable carriers of those same information categories, plus additional structure (typed directed edges with temporal scope; history-sensitive admissibility relation; event lineage with replay fidelity; context-indexed interpretation functions; component interfaces and cross-component identity).

- Architectural component assumption? No. P*/W6 nowhere require a five-way decomposition, component interfaces, or any particular carrier architecture; a package could satisfy P*/W6 in a radically different decomposition (single monolithic query engine, relational store, learned model with disclosed decoder — all within E*'s included families). W13 then constructs RCCD from the recovered information; the construction is where the architecture enters.
- Conclusion-equivalent premise? No clause literally asserts a component's existence; P*'s independence_controls ("contract_pass_does_not_imply_any_kernel", "clause_names_may_not_use_target_conclusion_vocabulary") are respected in the frozen texts.
- What the necessity lemmas actually prove: essentially "if the contract requires recovery of X-information, some recoverable, effective, equivalence-stable carrier of X-information must exist (with the lemma's extra antecedents)". That is informational necessity inherited largely from P*/W6, plus witness-structure content. Characterized accurately, W7–W11 are contract-relative reconstruction lemmas, not neutral discoveries of five architectural necessities.
- Anti-conclusion-loading controls test: the controls block lexical/definitional encoding of the terminal outcome and they hold; they do not (and cannot) make the necessity lemmas premise-independent. The frozen claim surfaces consistently label the lemmas "relative operational necessity only", and no frozen surface asserts neutral discovery — Lane C's LOGICAL_DEFECT classification attacks a stronger claim than the frozen theorem makes. Lane C's own countermodel demonstrates the loading is not full circularity: C-D1 satisfies P* while refuting a necessity lemma's witness, which would be impossible if the premises logically contained the conclusions.
