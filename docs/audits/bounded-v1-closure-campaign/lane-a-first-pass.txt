6. ADR-002 Classification

Classification: OFF_GRAPH_NONBLOCKING.

Dependency proof.

1. The exact proposition affected by A vs. B. Whether unresolved at frameworks/FARA/admissibility-structure.md:111 is (A) a substantive value inside the calculus-determined classification set S_K, or (B) a FARA-level meta-status outside S_K (Ω_K : C → S_K ∪ {⊥}). Consequences differ only in: redundancy of :111’s second disjunct, attachment of the Traceability (:117) and Auditability (:144) obligations to unresolved candidates, and satisfiability of :111 under calculi lacking an unresolved value.
2. Trace to bounded-v1. The bounded-v1 theorem’s admissibility content resides in exactly three places, none of which is FARA’s Ω: (i) E*‘s package-admissibility criteria E1–E6 with its own three-valued rule; (ii) P*‘s structural_fidelity clause requiring recoverability of the source system’s registered admissibility distinctions (whatever the source’s own semantics declares — not FARA’s); (iii) W8’s constructed history-sensitive admissibility relation, which is extensionally defined from registered query answers with its own effective_admissibility_decision_or_explicit_unknown discharge. Mechanical verification: zero occurrences of FARA, Ω/omega, or unresolved status in theory/class, theory/contract, theory/equivalence, theory/representations, theory/machinery at the frozen commit. The UPP chain’s third truth-values are separately defined per artifact (W1 undetermined, E* unknown, P* aggregation Unknown), and the frozen record itself establishes these are distinct concepts from FARA unresolved status (UQ-T20 four-way distinction — verified against the underlying texts, not merely cited).
3. Distinctions maintained. (a) Unresolved-status representation — pure FARA/Ω, off-graph. (b) Broader Ω/admissibility semantics — off-graph for the same reason; CE-ADM-001’s expressive-loss result targets Ω’s three-valued reading, not E*. (c) Theorem-relevant admissibility — E* and W8, both self-contained. (d) Downstream auditability/traceability — the A/B difference changes FARA’s obligations for unresolved candidates; no bounded-v1 artifact inherits those obligations.
4. Charter authorization. The Research Execution Charter provides no mechanism that lawfully selects between evidence-underdetermined alternatives: its Discovery Standard forbids preference/consensus/authority as evidence; its Human Judgment section mandates “Stop. Produce competing hypotheses. Do not guess.”; its lifecycle requires Replication → Acceptance → Promotion, which ADR-002 records as not having occurred. So if ADR-002 were on-graph, it would be an unresolved governance blocker — there is no existing authorized selection rule. This is why the off-graph proof matters and why the third label (ON_GRAPH_WITH_EXISTING_AUTHORIZED_SELECTION_RULE) would be wrong even as a fallback.
5. Counter-dependency test (§11 discipline). Select A: :111 becomes redundant; traceability/auditability attach; a tension with :84 arises. Not one token of C*, E*, P*, closure, equivalence, W7–W14 lemma statements, or the terminal conclusion changes, because none of them contains the affected term or imports Ω’s type. The interpretation of every required premise is likewise unchanged — the premises define their own three-valued semantics internally. “Documented accurately” was not used as “resolved”: ADR-002 remains a genuinely open architectural decision; it is nonblocking for bounded-v1 solely because bounded-v1 does not depend on the decided proposition.
