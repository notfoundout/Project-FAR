## Where they touch

FAR's boundary specification assigns to **FARA** the layer that owns *"representational
objects, states, transitions, admissibility architecture."* `FARA-FORMAL-KERNEL-001` is
recorded there as an *identity-bearing many-sorted relational carrier architecture with typed
occurrence-sensitive identity*, and it records that *"typed-hypergraph and algebraic/
state-transition forms remain derived views rather than upstream prerequisites."*

BIA lives on precisely that terrain, and two of its load-bearing moves are the same moves:

1. **Arity of the transition structure.** T1 shows that for MLL a binary state-transition form
   is inadequate and a hyperedge form is required. That is a statement about the relationship
   between the two forms FARA's kernel note names — and it is a case where the hypergraph form
   is *not* an optional derived view but the only adequate one.
2. **Occurrence-sensitivity.** T2's frontier construction works because the leaves of a
   derivation are *occurrences*, so duplicate open sequents remain distinct and a multiset —
   not a set — is the canonical image. FARA's kernel independently records typed
   occurrence-sensitive identity as constitutive.

That is genuine partial overlap on the question *when is a state/transition representation
adequate to what it represents*.

## Where they do not touch

- **Provenance is independent.** BIA was derived inside a closed dialogue-built experiment
  whose panel, grammars, source rules, and prediction were frozen without reference to FAR, and
  whose one substantive content result *falsifies its own preregistered prediction*. No FAR
  artifact was used as a premise. Conversely, nothing in BIA was derived from FARA.
- **Scope is incomparable.** BIA is proved for exactly two rule systems (propositional LK,
  MLL). FARA's kernel is recorded as Accepted for *"Project FAR v1.0 finite explicit auditable
  representational architecture."* Neither range contains the other, and BIA is by far the
  narrower.
- **BIA says nothing about FAR's central result.** The repository records a relative,
  operational, strictly-weakened universality theorem `POST-TUE-UPP-001`, together with a
  bounded-v1 cross-audit adjudicated `FROZEN_V1_NOT_REFUTED_BUT_NOT_ESTABLISHED`. BIA neither
  supports nor challenges any of that. It does not bear on the UPP premises, the dependency
  audit, the maximality boundary, or the successor repair program.
- **No transfer of status in either direction.** BIA's proofs are internal and unreviewed;
  they confer nothing on FAR. FAR's repository status confers nothing on BIA. Repository
  placement is not epistemic authority in either direction.

## One observation offered without any claim of authority

If BIA's criterion survives independent review, it would be a *candidate* falsifier-generator
for representational-adequacy claims of the kind FARA's layer makes: given a rule system with
conjunctive multi-premise rules, ask whether the proposed carrier internalises the branching,
and if not, exhibit the over-generation and under-generation countermodels as in T1.

This is offered as a possible future test, not as a finding, not as a dependency, and not as
evidence for or against anything currently registered in Project FAR. Acting on it would
require the ordinary registration, preregistration, and adversarial-gate machinery that
governs Project FAR research — none of which has been invoked here.
