## Evidentiary discipline used throughout

- All three transcript SHA-256 hashes were verified against the supplied manifest before use.
- **Turn 36 is absent from the supplied corpus.** Its content is used only as the closure
  prompt's non-verbatim summary, labelled `RESUME-SUMMARY`. No Turn 36 text was reconstructed
  or quoted.
- No source content is asserted from training recall. Where a passage was returned by a search
  invocation but its host could not be inspected, it is marked **attribution unverified** and
  is never used to ground a positive or negative certificate.
- Supplied primary documents are hashed, their bibliographic identity verified from their own
  front matter or embedded metadata, and their extraction limits disclosed per quotation
  (`[OCR]`, `[sic]`, bracketed restoration of lost typography).
- A supplied document already inside a frozen near-match set is inspected under the **original**
  procedure; one outside it is inspected only under the separately versioned `E0-supplied-v1`,
  and the frozen candidate set is never retrofitted.
- Claims withdrawn by self-audit are retained as **superseded**, with the reason, rather than
  deleted. Revision 1's BIA, its "iff", and its S6 UNDERDETERMINED verdict are so retained.
- No Codex-generated $U_0$, witness matrix, rank, or minimality claim has been consulted,
  imported, or used: it lacks the required K4 source record, and the precondition for revealing
  it — sealed independent extraction — has not been met.
- The frozen run's own defect (Turn 17's incorrect S6 determination check) is **preserved as
  observed**, not repaired.
- `OPEN` never counts as `NO`; novelty is never returned positive on an uncertified
  translation; failure, falsification, and `Unknown` are recorded as outcomes.
